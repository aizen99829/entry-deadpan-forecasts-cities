A make.fun entry: one line, at most 160 characters — a deadpan weather
forecast for a city that does not exist, delivered in the flat, serious
tone of a TV meteorologist.


## The entry

> Tonight in Velmaris, rain will fall upward at 14 degrees, with a northwesterly breeze of 12 kilometres per hour; hold umbrellas underneath until 3 a.m.

Velmaris is invented. The forecast reports a single impossible event —
rain falling upward — as if it were entirely routine, anchored by small
realistic details: a temperature, a wind direction and speed, and a time.
Nothing in the line explains the joke; the absurdity stands on its own.
The line is 151 characters long, inside the competition's 160-character
limit, and names no real city.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-line forecast to `/output/entry.txt`.
